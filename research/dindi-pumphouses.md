# Dindi Lift Irrigation Scheme — Pump House Research

## Summary
- Full name: Sri Ramaraju Vidyasagar Rao (R. Vidyasagar Rao) Dindi Lift Irrigation Scheme / Project (SRVR DLIS / DLIP).
- **Key finding: the scheme's own works do not include a dedicated high-lift pump house at Ulpara Barrage.** Per an official Aug-2024 Telangana Irrigation Department (Superintending Engineer, Irrigation Circle, Mall) presentation, the actual water-drawal point for DLIS was fixed (Govt Memo No. 1915/Proj-I/A1/2024, dt. 13.03.2024) at the **Veeranjaneya (Yedula) Reservoir of the Palamuru-Rangareddy LIS (PRLIS)** — i.e., DLIS draws water *after* PRLIS has already lifted it from the Srisailam foreshore. From Yedula, water reaches Ulpara Barrage and then Dindi's own main canal purely by **gravity** (open canal + a 16 km, 9 m-dia tunnel + stream), with no pump house identified for this "headworks" link in the available sources. [trea-vsr.org PPT]
- Source (Srisailam foreshore, ~270 m) → lifted by PRLIS's own pump houses (not part of DLIS) → Veeranjaneya/Yedula Reservoir (FRL +445.0 m, MDDL +436.25 m) → gravity link (tunnel + canal, 21.575 km) → Ulpara Barrage (FRL +412.0 m) → stream → Pothireddypally Checkdam on Dhundhibi river (~+412 m) → DLIS main canal (66.3 km, gravity-fed) → 9 reservoirs down to Shivannagudem Balancing Reservoir (FRL +385.6 m).
- Destination/terminus: Shivannagudem Balancing Reservoir, FRL +385.600 m (largest reservoir in the chain, capacity 11.968 TMC, command 155,000 acres). The "main" existing Dindi Reservoir sits mid-chain at FRL +396.545 m.
- Total lift within DLIS's own main-canal works: effectively **0 m** — it is a gravity cascade. All genuine lift (≈270 m → ≈445 m, roughly 175 m) is performed by PRLIS pump houses, which are outside DLIS's scope and not detailed in the sources reviewed here.
- Command area: 3.61 lakh acres (361,000 acres) net ayacut, across 21 mandals / 7 Assembly constituencies in 4 districts (Nagarkurnool, Nalgonda, Yadadri-Bhuvanagiri, Ranga Reddy), covering ~228 fluoride-affected villages.
- Status overview: Administratively sanctioned 2015 (Rs 6,190 Cr, G.O. Ms. No. 107 dt. 11.06.2015); work commenced 2016. As of June 2024, cumulative expenditure Rs 3,410.91 Cr of Rs 4,822.41 Cr contracted (7 packages); only ~12,000 of 361,000 acres of irrigation potential created. Several reservoirs stalled on land acquisition/R&R and environmental clearance; NGT (Southern Zone) ordered Rs 92.85 Cr environmental compensation and a construction halt (22.12.2022) pending EC — Telangana appealed to the Supreme Court (filed 20.03.2023), still pending as of the sources reviewed. As of 2025–2026, KRMB told NGT that Telangana had not yet submitted a DPR for DLIS, and it remains gazetted as a "major, ongoing, unapproved, non-operational" project. Not commissioned/operational as of the latest sources found (mid-2026).

## Pump Houses

No pump house was identified in any source for the core 30-TMC Dindi cascade itself (Ulpara Barrage → Pothireddypally → the 9 reservoirs above are gravity-fed). The only pump houses found in connection with the "Dindi" project family are three **small, separate lift schemes** administered by the same Irrigation Circle (Mall) office, drawing from the Nagarjuna Sagar foreshore (not Srisailam) to serve fluoride-affected villages in Neradugommu/Chandampet mandals, Nalgonda district. These appear to be bundled with DLIS administratively/geographically but are not part of the 12-reservoir/30-TMC network.

### Ambabhavani Lift Irrigation Scheme
- Location: Vizag Colony village, Neradugommu mandal, Nalgonda district.
- Source: Foreshore of Nagarjuna Sagar Reservoir (jackwell-cum-pump house; approach channel 2,100 m; MDDL/LWL +155.45 m).
- Number of pumping units: 2 (single stage).
- Unit capacity: 2 × 4.5 MW synchronous motors with vertical turbine (VT) pumps.
- Total installed capacity: 9 MW.
- Lift (head): 134 m.
- Discharge capacity: 157 cusecs.
- Command area: 13,048 acres (5,280 ha).
- Cost/status: Admin. sanction Rs 184.56 Cr (G.O.Rt.No.68, I&CAD (Project-I) Dept., dt. 09.02.2021); agreement value Rs 151.95 Cr (later Rs 151.95 Cr revised); financial progress ~20% (Rs 29 Cr) as of Aug 2024.
- Sources: [trea-vsr.org PPT]

### Kambalapally Lift Irrigation Scheme
- Location: Foreshore of Nagarjuna Sagar Reservoir near Kambalapally village, Chandampet mandal, Nalgonda district.
- Number of pumping stages: 2 (two separate pump houses in series).
  - Stage-I pump house: 2 × 3.5 MW motors, discharge 100 cusecs, head 163.00 m.
  - Stage-II pump house: 2 × 2.5 MW motors, discharge 65 cusecs, head 159.00 m.
- Total installed capacity: 12 MW (7 MW Stage-I + 5 MW Stage-II).
- Total discharge: 100 cusecs.
- Command area: 8,244 acres.
- Villages benefited: Kambalapally, Old Kambalapally, Yelamalamanda, Guvvalagutta, Kasarajpally.
- Cost/status: Admin. sanction G.O.Rt.No.68, I&CAD (Project-I) Dept., dt. 09.02.2021; project cost Rs 202.15 Cr, agreement value Rs 159.44 Cr; financial progress ~9.5% (Rs 15 Cr) as of Aug 2024.
- Sources: [trea-vsr.org PPT]

### Pogilla Lift Irrigation Scheme
- Location: On the Krishna River near Pogilla village, Chandampet mandal, Nalgonda district.
- Number of pumping units: 3 vertical turbine (VT) pumps.
- Unit capacity: 0.35 MW each.
- Total installed capacity: ~1.05 MW.
- Lift (head): 171.00 m.
- Discharge capacity: 14 cusecs.
- Command area: 1,119 acres.
- Cost/status: Admin. sanction G.O.Rt.No.68, dt. 09.02.2021; project cost Rs 24.56 Cr, agreement value Rs 20.02 Cr; work **not started** as of Aug 2024 (blocked on forest land).
- Sources: [trea-vsr.org PPT]

### "Ulpara Barrage" / Srisailam-foreshore intake for DLIS proper
- No named pump house, unit count, MW rating, or discharge/lift figures were found for a Dindi-dedicated pumping facility at Ulpara Barrage or on the Srisailam foreshore. The headworks linking PRLIS's Yedula Reservoir to Ulpara Barrage (0.8 km approach channel + 2.525 km open canal + 16 km, 9 m-dia tunnel + 3.05 km open canal + a natural stream reach) are gravity works, with a still-unapproved cost estimate of Rs 1,788.89 Cr submitted to the Telangana government (status: awaiting approval as of Aug 2024). No pumps are shown on this stretch in the department's own schematic.
- **Not found in available sources**: any independent Dindi pump house at/near Srisailam or Ulpara Barrage separate from PRLIS's own pumping infrastructure.

## Data conflicts / uncertainty
- **Number of reservoirs**: Widely repeated web/Wikipedia-adjacent summaries state "7 offline reservoirs and 5 online reservoirs" (12 total) in the DLIS network. The official Aug-2024 department presentation's own reservoir table lists only 9 (Pothireddypally checkdam + 8 new/existing reservoirs), and a May-2026 news report corroborates "four online and five offline reservoirs" (9 total, 25.31 TMC). The 12-reservoir figure could not be corroborated with a matching list of names; it may double-count headworks structures (e.g., Ulpara Barrage, Yedula Reservoir) as part of "Dindi," or may reflect an earlier/different project configuration.
- **Source/lift framing**: The existing app data frames DLIS as lifting water directly from "Srisailam foreshore (Ulpara Barrage intake)" at 270 m elevation. Research indicates Ulpara Barrage itself sits at +412 m FRL — far above Srisailam's ~270 m water level — because it receives water only after PRLIS has already lifted it to the Yedula Reservoir (+445 m FRL). DLIS's own capital works, from Ulpara Barrage onward, are gravity-fed (net fall of roughly 15–27 m to the various reservoirs, not a lift). The "30 TMC lift" figure describes the water PRLIS pumps up for eventual DLIS use, not lift performed by DLIS's own pump houses. This is a substantive correction to the assumption that DLIS itself operates the intake pump house.
- **Dindi Reservoir capacity**: Wikipedia states the existing Dindi Reservoir's gross storage is "59 million cubic meters" (≈2.08 TMC), matching the app's existing `reservoirs.json` figure (2.08 TMC). The Aug-2024 department table instead lists 2.468 TMC for "Dindi Reservoir (Existing)." Both are plausible (gross vs. live/other capacity definition) but were not reconciled.
- **Command area**: Cited consistently as 3.61 lakh (361,000) acres net ayacut in most sources; one Hans India article rounds this to "3.5 lakh acres."
- **Commissioning/operational status**: No source describes DLIS as commissioned or operational. It remains under construction with multiple reservoirs stalled on land acquisition, R&R, forest clearance, and — per KRMB's 2025/2026 submission to NGT — DPR approval. Treat as "under construction, not operational" as of the most recent sources (2026).
- **Total lift / destination elevation** (the fields flagged as unconfirmed in the existing project data): based on this research, the correct characterization is that DLIS's own works involve **no significant lift** — treat `sourceElevation_m` (270 m, Srisailam) as the elevation of PRLIS's true intake rather than DLIS's, and note that DLIS's actual intake (Ulpara Barrage, +412 m) to its terminus (Shivannagudem, +385.6 m) is a gravity decline, not a lift.
