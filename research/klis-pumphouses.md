# Kalwakurthy Lift Irrigation Scheme (KLIS) — Pump House Research

Official name: **Mahatma Gandhi Kalwakurthy Lift Irrigation Scheme (MGKLIS)**. Krishna-basin scheme in erstwhile Mahbubnagar district (today mainly Nagarkurnool district), Telangana — not to be confused with the Godavari-basin Kaleshwaram Lift Irrigation Scheme (also sometimes shortened "KLIS").

## Summary
- Source: Srisailam reservoir foreshore at **Regumangadda, Yellur (V), Kollapur mandal** — level **+244.40 m**. [krmb.gov.in](https://krmb.gov.in/html/project18.html)
- Destination: level **+502.00 m** (figure repeated consistently across sources — see conflicts section below for reconciliation). [krmb.gov.in](https://krmb.gov.in/html/project18.html)
- Total lift: cited as **298 m** in essentially all secondary sources; one aggregator breaks this down stage-wise as 95 m + 86 m + 117 m = 298 m (unverified against a primary government document — see conflicts). The straight-line elevation gap between the quoted source (244.4 m) and destination (502.0 m) figures is **257.6 m**, ~40 m less than the quoted 298 m gross lift.
- Design capacity: **25 TMC/year** lifted from Srisailam (original DPR: 20 TMC, later enhanced; some sources mention 40 TMC).
- Command area: **3.40–3.65 lakh acres** (figures vary by source/date) across Nagarkurnool, Wanaparthy, Mahbubnagar and Rangareddy (Shamshabad) districts. One breakdown: Nagarkurnool 268,165 acres, Wanaparthy 78,000 acres, Mahbubnagar 9,015 acres, Shamshabad 9,820 acres (≈3.65 lakh acres total). [scribd.com/document/542530957](https://www.scribd.com/document/542530957/mgklis-details)
- Also supplies drinking water to ~300–336 villages in the erstwhile Kollapur, Nagarkurnool, Achampet, Jadcherla and Kalwakurthy taluks.
- Cost: ₹2,100 crore (per Wikipedia infobox); construction began 1984 (foundation stone), restarted 2014, main works inaugurated **16 October 2017**.
- Status overview: Formally opened October 2017; individual lifts were commissioned in stages starting with Lift-I (Yellur/Regumangadda) around September 2012. As of a Telangana Today report on PRLIS integration, the scheme has been running **under-utilized** — of the 5×30 MW pumps installed per stage, typically only 3 were run regularly (a 4th intermittently) because the main canal's carrying capacity is well below the pumps' design discharge. A canal integration with the Palamuru–Rangareddy Lift Irrigation Scheme (PRLIS) has been proposed to fix this bottleneck. [telanganatoday.com](https://telanganatoday.com/integration-of-prlis-with-kalwakurthy-lift-on-the-anvil)

## Balancing reservoirs (in order)
1. **Yellur** — 0.35 TMC
2. **Singotam** — 0.55 TMC
3. **Jonnalaboguda** (Jonnala Bogada) — 2.14 TMC
4. **Gudipally Gattu** (Gudipallygattu) — 0.96 TMC

Total 4 balancing reservoirs, connected via 10.956 km of tunnels, 1.60 km of approach channel and 29 km of gravity canals. From Gudipally Gattu the water continues via a ~100 km gravity main canal towards Kalwakurthy and the wider command area. [The Hans India](https://www.thehansindia.com/telangana/water-from-kalwakurthy-li-project-released-553452), [scribd.com/document/542530957](https://www.scribd.com/document/542530957/mgklis-details)

Note: reporting on the exact routing is not fully consistent — one account has Lift-I discharging into *both* Yellur and Singotam, with Lift-II raising water into Jonnalaboguda and Lift-III into Gudipally Gattu; another account (Hans India) describes Yellur → (gravity/canal) → Singotam → Lift-II → Jonnalaboguda → Lift-III → gravity canal from Gudipally onward. The reservoir list and 3-lift structure are consistent across sources; the precise gravity-vs-pumped segments between reservoirs are not fully reconcilable from available sources.

## Pump Houses

### Lift-I / Pump House I — Regumangadda (Yellur)
- Location: Regumangadda, Yellur village, Kollapur mandal, Nagarkurnool district (intake at Srisailam foreshore)
- Stage number: 1
- Number of pumping units: 5 vertical turbine (Francis-type) pumps
- Unit capacity (MW): 30 MW each — described in contractor documentation as "the largest pump capacity installed in [undivided] Andhra Pradesh" at the time
- Total installed capacity (MW): 150 MW
- Lift (m): static head ~90 m per a contractor (Patel Engineering) project page for this package; one aggregator source states 95 m for this stage
- Discharge capacity: ~800 cusecs (22.66 cumecs per pump per contractor spec, ×5 pumps ≈ matches 800 cusecs total quoted for stages 1–2)
- Commissioned / status: First lift partially commissioned ~September 2012 (initial supply to ~12,000 acres); full scheme (all 3 lifts) operational by October 2017. As of latest reporting, typically 3 of 5 pumps run regularly.
- Notes: Pump house dimensions reported as 72.60 m × 18.60 m × 69.0 m; underground tunnel 388.5 m (7.0 m dia., horseshoe); surge pool 110 m × 40 m × 64.61 m; delivery mains 1,420 m (3.0 m dia. MS pipe). Feeds Yellur (and per some sources also Singotam) balancing reservoir(s).
- Sources: [pateleng.com](https://pateleng.com/project-inner.php?projects=118), [krmb.gov.in](https://krmb.gov.in/html/project18.html), [thehansindia.com](https://www.thehansindia.com/telangana/water-from-kalwakurthy-li-project-released-553452), [en.wikipedia.org](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahatma_Gandhi_Kalwakurthy_lift_irrigation_scheme)

### Lift-II / Pump House II — Jonnalaboguda
- Location: Jonnalaboguda village, Nagarkurnool district
- Stage number: 2
- Number of pumping units: 5 (same design family as Lift-I)
- Unit capacity (MW): 30 MW each
- Total installed capacity (MW): 150 MW
- Lift (m): not independently confirmed from a primary source; one aggregator figure states 86 m for this stage
- Discharge capacity: ~800 cusecs (same as Lift-I per most sources)
- Commissioned / status: Operational as part of the full scheme by 2017; reported to have run normally even during a 2019 Lift-III outage (see below)
- Notes: Raises water into the Jonnalaboguda balancing reservoir (2.14 TMC, the largest of the four). Tunnel segment for this stage reported as 4.553 km, 6.85 m diameter, D-type.
- Sources: [en.wikipedia.org](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahatma_Gandhi_Kalwakurthy_lift_irrigation_scheme), [thehansindia.com — third lift snag](https://www.thehansindia.com/telangana/motors-at-kalwakurthy-lift-irrigation-third-lift-face-technical-snag-pumping-of-water-halted-554267)

### Lift-III / Pump House III — Gudipally
- Location: Gudipally village, Nagarkurnool district
- Stage number: 3
- Number of pumping units: 5 (same design family)
- Unit capacity (MW): 30 MW each
- Total installed capacity (MW): 150 MW
- Lift (m): not independently confirmed from a primary source; one aggregator figure states 117 m for this stage (the largest single-stage lift, needed to reach the final +502 m level); discharge is correspondingly lower for this stage
- Discharge capacity: ~650 cusecs (lower than stages 1–2, consistent with the higher head at this stage)
- Commissioned / status: Operational by 2017; suffered a documented outage on 12 August 2019 due to "a technical snag in the switching system of the pumphouse," halting pumping for ~2 days while Lifts I and II continued running; repairs directed by local MLA Marri Janardhan Reddy.
- Notes: Discharges into Gudipally Gattu balancing reservoir (0.96 TMC), from which a gravity canal carries water ~100 km toward Kalwakurthy and the wider command area. Tunnel segment reported as 6.15 km, 6.85 m diameter, D-type.
- Sources: [thehansindia.com — third lift snag](https://www.thehansindia.com/telangana/motors-at-kalwakurthy-lift-irrigation-third-lift-face-technical-snag-pumping-of-water-halted-554267), [en.wikipedia.org](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahatma_Gandhi_Kalwakurthy_lift_irrigation_scheme)

## Data conflicts / uncertainty
- **Total lift 298 m vs. elevation-gap 257.6 m**: Nearly every source (Wikipedia, KRMB, aggregators, tender docs) repeats "244.40 m → 502.00 m, lifted 298 m in 3 stages" as a single fixed phrase, which is internally inconsistent (502.00 − 244.40 = 257.60, not 298). A stage-by-stage breakdown found via a secondary aggregator (95 m + 86 m + 117 m = 298 m) is numerically self-consistent and plausible (it's common for quoted "total lift"/gross head figures to include pump/friction losses, operating-level margins in balancing reservoirs, and surge allowances beyond the simple endpoint-elevation subtraction), but this breakdown could **not** be verified against a primary Telangana Irrigation Department or KRMB document in the sources checked. Treat the 95/86/117 m split as **unverified/low-confidence**; the 298 m gross-lift figure and the 244.40 m / 502.00 m endpoint elevations are each independently well-attested but do not arithmetically reconcile with each other in any source found.
- **Command area figures vary by source and date**: 3.40 lakh acres (137,000 ha) is the original DPR ayacut figure quoted in most infobox-style sources; 3.65 lakh acres appears in reservoir/status-focused sources (possibly reflecting a later enhancement from 20 TMC to 25 TMC design capacity). An early appraisal document (via Scribd) instead cites 2.5 lakh acres and a total lift of "245 m" — this appears to be a superseded, earlier-stage DPR figure rather than the "as-built" scheme, and is flagged here rather than used in the summary above.
- **TMC capacity**: Most sources say 25 TMC; the Wikipedia article separately states "original design 20 TMC; later increased to 40 TMC" — the 40 TMC figure looks anomalously high relative to every other source (which converge on 20→25 TMC) and could not be corroborated elsewhere; treated as likely error or referring to a different/combined scheme figure.
- **Routing between balancing reservoirs**: as noted above, sources disagree on whether Lift-I feeds both Yellur and Singotam directly, or whether Singotam is filled by gravity/canal flow from Yellur before Lift-II. The 4-reservoir list and overall Lift-I → Lift-II → Lift-III sequence is consistent; the precise pump-vs-gravity segments are not.
- **Per-stage lift heights and discharge for Lifts II and III**: no primary source found gives independently-sourced, stage-specific lift-height or discharge figures for Lift-II/III beyond the aggregator-derived 86 m / 117 m breakdown and the general "650 cusecs in third stage" figure; these should be treated as provisional pending a primary DPR/CWC document.
- **Cost and commissioning history**: Wikipedia's infobox states construction began 1984, restarted 2014 with ₹2,100 crore budget, opened 16 October 2017 — but other sources describe Lift-I as commissioned already in September 2012, implying a longer phased-commissioning timeline than the infobox alone suggests.
