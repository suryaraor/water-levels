# Kaleshwaram Stage 2 — Pump House Research (Mid Manair → Ananthagiri → Ranganayaka Sagar → Mallannasagar → Kondapochamma, + Gouravelli branch)

## Summary

- Source: Mid Manair Dam / MMD (318 m)
- Destination: Kondapochamma Sagar (618 m per project literature; one secondary source gives 510 m MSL — see Data Conflicts)
- Total lift across stage: ~300 m (MMD → Kondapochamma), plus a separate ~170 m lift from Yellampally into MMD
- Status overview: All pump houses described below were built under the original 2016–2019 KLIS construction push and have pumped water at least once (several achieved 100+ TMC lifetime cumulative pumping milestones by 2020–2021). Several stations (Kannepalli/Lakshmi, Annaram/Saraswathi, Sundilla/Parvathi — all on the separate Link-1 chain, see note below) were badly flooded/damaged in the July 2022 Godavari floods and required repair; press coverage of flood damage to the Link-2/Link-3/Link-4 stations covered in this report (Nandi, Gayatri, Annapurna, Ranganayakasagar, Mallannasagar, Akkaram, Markook, Regonda) was not found, so their post-2022 operational status is unconfirmed beyond general "pumps resumed" reporting for the project as a whole.
- **Important naming correction**: The pump-house names "Ramadugu," "Kannepalli," and "Gandhari" used in the app's current data do not all refer to the Yellampally→Mid Manair chain. Research indicates the *official* KLIS names for the two pump houses that actually lift water from Yellampally to Mid Manair Dam (Link-2, Packages 6–8) are the **Nandi (Nandimedaram) Pump House** and the **Gayatri Pump House** (located in Ramadugu mandal — hence the "Ramadugu" label is a location reference to Gayatri, not a separate third pump house). **"Kannepalli" is a real KLIS pump house (official name "Lakshmi"), but it sits on the earlier, separate Link-1 chain (Medigadda → Annaram → Sundilla → Yellampally), not between Yellampally and Mid Manair.** No pump house named "Gandhari" could be found in any source consulted — see Data Conflicts.

## Pump Houses

### Nandi Pump House (Nandimedaram)
- Location: Nandimedaram, Package-6/7 of Link-2, en route from Sripada Yellampalli reservoir toward Mid Manair Dam (Karimnagar/Jagtial belt); precise mandal not confirmed in sources reviewed.
- Associated lift step: Yellampally (148 m) → Nandimedaram surge pool/reservoir (intermediate stage before Mid Manair)
- Number of pumping units: 7
- Unit capacity (MW): ~124–124.5 MW each ("Bahubali" motors, BHEL-supplied)
- Total installed capacity (MW): ~871–875 MW (7 × 124.5)
- Lift (m): reported as both ~105 m and ~110 m in different articles (not fully reconciled)
- Discharge capacity: not found in available sources (specific cusecs/TMC figure for this station not located)
- Pump/motor type: vertical-shaft, high-capacity motor-pump sets, BHEL-manufactured
- Commissioned / status: Renamed "Nandi Barrage and Pump House" at the August 2019 goddess-naming ceremony; operational since 2019; pumps reported "roaring back into action" from Nandi Medaram after a maintenance/outage period (exact year of that report not confirmed).
- Notes: This is very likely the pump house the app's dataset intends by the "Kannepalli" label in the Yellampally→MMD leg, given it is the first-stage lift on that route — but its real official name is Nandi/Nandimedaram, not Kannepalli.
- Sources: https://telanganatoday.com/kaleshwaram-pumps-roar-back-into-action-operations-begin-from-nandi-medaram ; https://www.thehansindia.com/telangana/barrages-pump-houses-under-kaleshwaram-named-after-goddesses-553846 ; general WebSearch aggregation of Deccan Herald/Deccan Chronicle coverage.

### Gayatri Pump House (a.k.a. "Ramadugu")
- Location: Laxmipur (Lakshmipur) village, Ramadugu mandal, Karimnagar district
- Associated lift step: final stage of Link-2, delivering water into Mid Manair Dam
- Number of pumping units: 7
- Unit capacity (MW): 139 MW each (also reported as "140 MWh" pumps by BHEL in some press pieces — treat 139 MW as the more consistently cited figure)
- Total installed capacity (MW): 973 MW (7 × 139)
- Lift (m): 111 m (one source states ~110 m)
- Discharge capacity: 3,150 cusecs per machine (≈22,000 cusecs combined); overall station capacity cited as "2 TMC of water per day"
- Pump/motor type: Vertical, four-floor underground installation
- Commissioned / status: Named "Gayatri" at the August 2019 ceremony (previously "Lakshmipuram Pump House"); began operations around August 2019; reported record of ~100 TMC cumulative lift reached in a short period after commissioning.
- Notes: Multiple sources (MEIL's own site, The Hans India, PR Newswire) describe this as **the world's largest underground pump house** ("no other pump house in the world has pumping machines of this capacity"), built ~178 m below the surface across four floors. This claim is repeated consistently across MEIL press material and independent press (Hans India, Tupaki, PR Newswire) — it appears to be a genuine, actively-marketed engineering claim rather than a one-off assertion, though it originates largely from MEIL (the EPC contractor) rather than an independent engineering body.
- Sources: https://meil.in/gayatri-pumphouse ; https://www.thehansindia.com/business/worlds-biggest-pumphouse-inaugurated-at-lakshmipur-539945 ; https://www.prnewswire.com/in/news-releases/meil-s-worlds-biggest-pumphouse-inaugurated-871066891.html ; https://www.thehansindia.com/telangana/kaleshwaram-lifted-100tmc-water-by-each-pump-house-677112

### Lakshmi Pump House ("Kannepalli") — NOTE: this is on Link-1, not the Yellampally→MMD chain
- Location: Kannepalli village, adjacent to the Medigadda (Lakshmi) Barrage
- Associated lift step: Medigadda Barrage → Annaram Barrage (first stage of Link-1), i.e. upstream of Yellampally, not between Yellampally and Mid Manair
- Number of pumping units: 17
- Unit capacity (MW): 40 MW each (one Hans India article's figure of "139 MW, 2,376 tonnes per motor" for Kannepalli appears to be an error/conflation with the Gayatri pump specifications; the 40 MW figure is corroborated by multiple independent sources, so is treated as correct here)
- Total installed capacity (MW): 680 MW (17 × 40)
- Lift (m): 49 m
- Discharge capacity: cited capacity to pump ~3,000 million cubic feet (≈3 TMC) of water per day
- Pump/motor type: heavy-duty vertical motor pumps
- Commissioned / status: Inaugurated around July 6, 2019. Badly flooded in the July 2022 Godavari floods (at least 3 of 17 motors seriously damaged, 1 moderately damaged per Deccan Chronicle); repair cost reporting followed.
- Notes: The app's existing data appears to have conflated this genuine, well-documented "Kannepalli" pump house (Link-1) with the Yellampally→Mid Manair leg (Link-2). Recommend either dropping "Kannepalli" from the MMD chain or relabeling it as Nandi/Nandimedaram.
- Sources: https://www.deccanchronicle.com/nation/in-other-news/170822/huge-cost-to-get-kannepalli-and-annaram-pump-houses-back-in-action.html ; https://telanganatoday.com/brs-leader-vinod-kumar-urges-immediate-operation-of-kannepalli-pump-house-to-save-farmers

### "Gandhari" Pump House — NOT FOUND
- No source consulted (Wikipedia, MEIL, Telangana Today, Hans India, Deccan Chronicle/Herald, PR Newswire, CAG report references, X/Twitter threads) named a Kaleshwaram pump house called "Gandhari." Gandhari is a known place name in the Rajanna Sircilla/Karimnagar belt near Mid Manair, so it is possible the label refers to a canal/delivery point or village near the MMD inlet rather than a distinct pump house — but this could not be confirmed. Treat "Gandhari" as unverified/likely not a real, separate pump house; the Yellampally→MMD lift appears to be a two-stage chain (Nandi → Gayatri), not three-stage.
- Sources: not found in available sources.

- Location: Akkaram village, Gajwel mandal, Siddipet district

- Number of pumping units: 6
- Unit capacity (MW): 27 MW each
- Total installed capacity (MW): 162 MW — **matches the app's existing 6×27 MW figure; VERIFIED CORRECT** across multiple independent sources
- Lift (m): not found in available sources (project background's ~61 m figure is attributed to the combined Mallannasagar→Kondapochamma lift, which per this research is actually split across Akkaram + Markook, so ~61 m may need to be apportioned between the two stations rather than assigned solely to Akkaram)
- Discharge capacity: not found in available sources
- Pump/motor type: not found in available sources
- Commissioned / status: operational; part of KLIS Package-14 (Kondapochamma Sagar package)

The app's data currently treats "Akkaram (6×27 MW)" as the single pump house completing the Mallannasagar→Kondapochamma lift — research indicates there is a second station (Markook) in between that should probably also be represented.

## Data conflicts / uncertainty

1. **"Ramadugu / Kannepalli / Gandhari" naming**: These three labels, as used in the app's existing dataset for the Yellampally→Mid Manair leg, do not cleanly match KLIS's own naming. "Ramadugu" is a mandal name that houses the real **Gayatri** pump house; "Kannepalli" is a real KLIS pump house but sits on the separate, earlier Link-1 chain (Medigadda→Annaram), not on the Yellampally→MMD leg; "Gandhari" could not be found in any source and may not be a genuine, separate pump house. The best-supported real structure for Yellampally→Mid Manair is a **two-station chain**: Nandi (Nandimedaram) Pump House → Gayatri Pump House.
2. **Lift figures for Nandi Pump House**: cited as both ~105 m and ~110 m across different articles; not reconciled.
3. **Gayatri pump unit capacity**: cited as both "139 MW" (most common) and "140 MWh" (Business Standard/PTI wire language, which is dimensionally odd for a continuous-duty pump rating) — treated 139 MW as the primary figure.
4. **Ranganayakasagar pump house lift of 117 m** conflicts with the project background's assumption of only ~20 m for the Ananthagiri→Ranganayaka Sagar hop; it's unclear from available sources whether this pump house actually spans a longer combined lift (e.g., directly from Mid Manair or Annapurna's outlet) rather than the short single hop assumed in the background. Only found in one source (a Twitter/X thread aggregator), so treat with caution pending a second source.
5. **Mallannasagar "8 machines / 43 MW / 344 MW total"** vs. a separately reported "18 machines" figure attributed to a "Mallannasagar Reservoir Station-8" component — these do not reconcile and may refer to different sub-stations or be a reporting/OCR error in one of the two secondary sources.
6. **Kondapochamma Sagar elevation**: project background states 618 m; one secondary source (a blog aggregating government figures) states the reservoir sits at "510 meters MSL." Not reconciled — 618 m is more consistent with the project's overall stated ~300 m lift from Mid Manair (318 m), so is likely the more reliable figure, but this was not independently confirmed against a primary/official source.
7. **Akkaram/Markook vs. single "Akkaram (6×27 MW)" lift into Kondapochamma**: the app's current data models this as one pump house completing the full Mallannasagar→Kondapochamma lift. Research indicates it is actually a two-stage lift (Akkaram then Markook, KLIS Package-14), with Akkaram capacity (162 MW) confirmed correct but incomplete as a description of the full lift.
8. **CAG audit report PDFs** (cag.gov.in, Chapters IV and V of the 2024 Kaleshwaram compliance audit) could not be parsed for pump-house-level tables via the tools available in this research session — they were fetched but returned as encoded/unparseable binary content rather than readable text tables. A manual review of these PDFs (available at the URLs below) would likely be the single best source to resolve most of the conflicts above, particularly discharge capacities and lift heights, which were the weakest-sourced data points overall.
9. **Discharge capacity (cusecs/TMC) figures** are largely missing for Nandi, Annapurna, Ranganayakasagar, Mallannasagar, Akkaram, Markook, and Regonda pump houses — only Gayatri (3,150 cusecs/machine, 2 TMC/day) had a clearly sourced figure.
10. **Post-2022-flood operational status** of the Link-2/3/4 stations covered here (as opposed to the well-documented Link-1 stations Kannepalli/Annaram/Sundilla) is not clearly confirmed in the sources reviewed; general reporting suggests project-wide pumping resumed but station-by-station confirmation was not found.
