# Palamuru-Rangareddy Lift Irrigation Scheme (PRLIS) — Pump House Research

## Summary
- **Source**: Srisailam reservoir foreshore near Yellur village, Nagarkurnool district (~270 m elevation per project design; one source states "+240 m" — see Data conflicts).
- **Destination**: K.P. Lakshmidevipally reservoir near Shadnagar, FRL **+670.00 m** ([Telangana Today](https://telanganatoday.com/palamuru-rangareddy-lift-irrigation-scheme-mammoth-project-in-making), [pib/EAC minutes via search]).
- **Total lift**: 270 m → 670 m = **400 m** across 5 stages, matching the `liftReported_m: 400` already in `chains.json`.
- **Command area**: **12.3 lakh acres (~4,97,976 ha)** across Mahabubnagar, Nagarkurnool, Rangareddy, Vikarabad and Nalgonda districts ([The South First](https://thesouthfirst.com/telangana/palamuru-rangareddy-lift-irrigation-scheme-in-telangana-gets-new-deadline-december-2027/), [Telangana Today](https://telanganatoday.com/telangana-scripts-history-again-with-palamuru-rangareddy-lift-irrigation-scheme)). Earlier reports cite a smaller original ayacut of 10 lakh acres later raised to 12.03–12.3 lakh acres.
- **Status overview (latest available, mid-2025 sources)**: Phase I (Narlapur/Stage-I) was inaugurated 16 Sept 2023 with only 1 of 8–9 pumps operational at the time. As of Rabi 2025, Anjanagiri (Stage 1) reservoir was fully operational and partially-completed Veeranjaneya (Stage 2) and Venkatadri (Stage 3) reservoirs had begun irrigating parts of Nagarkurnool and Mahabubnagar districts. The scheme as a whole remains far from complete; the state government revised the completion deadline from December 2026 to **December 2027**, citing funding gaps after the Union government declined national-project status. Total project cost has escalated from an original ₹35,200 crore sanction to a projected **₹65,506 crore** (₹32,305 crore already spent under the previous government, plus an additional ₹33,201 crore estimated to finish) ([The South First](https://thesouthfirst.com/telangana/palamuru-rangareddy-lift-irrigation-scheme-in-telangana-gets-new-deadline-december-2027/)).

## Reservoirs / Stages (in order)
| Stage | Pump house | From reservoir (elev.) | To reservoir (elev., FRL) | Village/mandal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lift 1 | Narlapur (Yellur intake) | Srisailam foreshore (~270 m) | **Anjanagiri** (Narlapur), 345 m | Nagarkurnool dist. |
| Lift 2 | Yedula (Edula) | Anjanagiri, 345 m | **Sri Veeranjaneya** (Yedula), 445 m | Nagarkurnool dist. |
| Lift 3 | Vattem | Veeranjaneya, 445 m | **Venkatadri** (Vattem), 542 m *and* **Kurumurthyraya** (Karvena), 531 m | Nagarkurnool/Mahbubnagar border |
| Lift 4 | Udandapur (draws from Kurumurthyraya) | Kurumurthyraya, 531 m | **Udandapur**, 629 m | Jadcherla mandal, Mahbubnagar dist. |
| Lift 5 | K.P. Lakshmidevipally (near Shadnagar) | Udandapur, 629 m | **K.P. Lakshmidevipally**, 670 m | near Shadnagar, Rangareddy dist. |

Reservoir elevations per a compilation of Telangana Irrigation Dept./EAC figures reproduced in secondary sources ([Hans India — "TS Irrigation Projects: KCR-Made Wonders"](https://www.thehansindia.com/hans/opinion/news-analysis/ts-irrigation-projects-kcr-made-wonders-826612), [Telangana Today](https://telanganatoday.com/palamuru-rangareddy-reservoirs-reflect-legacy-of-kakatiyas-visionary-support-of-kcr)). Note Kurumurthyraya (531 m) is *lower* than the parallel Venkatadri reservoir (542 m) even though both are filled at Stage 3 — Lift 4 continues from Kurumurthyraya only, while Venkatadri/Vattem appears to function as a distributary balancing reservoir for local ayacut rather than continuing the main lift chain. Six reservoirs are named in total (Anjanagiri, Veeranjaneya, Venkatadri, Kurumurthyraya, Udandapur, K.P. Lakshmidevipally) against 5 lift stages, consistent with this branch. Total combined reservoir storage: **67.52 TMC** ([Deccan Chronicle](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/nation/current-affairs/150923/prlis-set-for-inauguration-despite-being-off-full-capacity.html)); Kurumurthyraya/Karvena alone is reported as the largest at ~19 TMC, creating new ayacut of ~1.5 lakh acres.

## Pump Houses

### Stage I — Narlapur Pump House
- **Location**: Narlapur village, near Yellur, Nagarkurnool district (draws from Srisailam foreshore).
- **Number of pumping units**: Reported inconsistently as **8 pumps** ([newstap.in](https://www.newstap.in/telangana/prlis-machines-can-pump-3000-cusecs-in-one-second-bigger-than-kaleshwaram-1494547), Wikipedia-derived summary) or **9 pumps** ([Telangana Today, "Stage-I pumps ready for dry run"](https://telanganatoday.com/telangana-prlis-stage-i-pumps-ready-for-dry-run)). Only 1 pump was operational at the September 2023 inauguration.
- **Unit capacity**: 145 MW each — supplied by BHEL, described as larger than the 139 MW "Bahubali" motors at Kaleshwaram's Ramadugu pump house.
- **Total installed capacity**: ~1,160–1,305 MW (8×145 or 9×145, depending on source).
- **Lift height**: Reservoir-elevation difference implies ~75 m (270 m→345 m); however one Telangana Today report describes pumps lifting water "from a depth of 9.20 metres," which appears to describe pump submergence/suction depth rather than total static lift — treat with caution.
- **Discharge capacity**: ~23,166 cusecs total design discharge cited for the stage (~2,570 cusecs/pump), designed to move ~2 TMC/day.
- **Commissioned / status**: Phase I inaugurated 16 Sept 2023 (dry run); by Rabi 2025 the stage and Anjanagiri reservoir were described as operational.
- **Sources**: [Telangana Today – dry run](https://telanganatoday.com/dry-run-of-narlapur-pump-in-prlis-likely-by-august-end); [Telangana Today – Stage I ready](https://telanganatoday.com/telangana-prlis-stage-i-pumps-ready-for-dry-run); [Deccan Chronicle](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/nation/current-affairs/150923/prlis-set-for-inauguration-despite-being-off-full-capacity.html); [newstap.in](https://www.newstap.in/telangana/prlis-machines-can-pump-3000-cusecs-in-one-second-bigger-than-kaleshwaram-1494547)

### Stage II — Yedula (Edula) Pump House
- **Location**: Yedula village, Nagarkurnool district; feeds Sri Veeranjaneya reservoir.
- **Number of pumping units**: **10 pumps** (reported as "9+1" — 9 duty + 1 standby).
- **Unit capacity**: 145 MW each.
- **Total installed capacity**: ~1,450 MW.
- **Lift height**: ~100 m by reservoir-elevation difference (345→445 m); a Telangana Today figure separately cites **124 m** lift for the Yedula pump house — discrepancy noted below.
- **Discharge capacity**: ~2,650 cusecs/pump cited in one source (aggregate not clearly stated); reservoir capacity ~6.5 TMC.
- **Commissioned / status**: Under construction/partially complete; described as helping irrigate parts of Nagarkurnool/Mahabubnagar in Rabi 2025 despite incomplete status. Also noted for housing "India's largest underground cavern" for a pump house (415 m long, 30 m wide, 90 m deep) per The South First.
- **Sources**: [The South First](https://thesouthfirst.com/telangana/palamuru-rangareddy-lift-irrigation-scheme-in-telangana-gets-new-deadline-december-2027/); [Telangana Today – mammoth project](https://telanganatoday.com/palamuru-rangareddy-lift-irrigation-scheme-mammoth-project-in-making); [suryaa.com – Edula works](https://suryaa.com/telangana-english-1525-)

### Stage III — Vattem Pump House
- **Location**: Vattem village, Nagarkurnool/Mahbubnagar border; fills Venkatadri (Vattem) reservoir and, per project design, continues onward via Kurumurthyraya (Karvena) reservoir.
- **Number of pumping units**: **10 pumps** ("9+1") per Telangana Today; a Deccan Chronicle flood-damage report (Oct 2020) describes only **4 motors fixed, a 5th being installed** at the time — i.e., construction was far from complete then.
- **Unit capacity**: 145 MW each.
- **Total installed capacity**: ~1,450 MW (design).
- **Lift height**: ~86–97 m by elevation difference depending on which downstream reservoir is used for the calc (445→531 m or 445→542 m); one source separately cites **121 m** for Vattem — discrepancy noted below.
- **Discharge capacity**: ~2,650 cusecs/pump cited (same figure as Stage II/IV in aggregated reporting — possibly a generic figure applied across stages by that source).
- **Commissioned / status**: Construction hit significant delays; the pump house was flooded twice during monsoon (2020, and again per "Vattem Pump House Too Goes Underwater"), causing damage estimated with Kaleshwaram flood damage at ~₹700 crore combined. Reported partially operational for Rabi 2025 irrigation via Venkatadri reservoir despite incomplete status.
- **Sources**: [Deccan Chronicle – Vattem flooded](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/southern-states/telangana/another-pump-house-flooded-this-time-its-the-palamuru-rangareddy-project-1821163); [Telangana Today – Vattem flooding](https://telanganatoday.com/telangana-prlis-vattem-pump-house-affected-by-flooding); [Telangana Today – infrastructure damage](https://telanganatoday.com/telangana-major-infrastructural-damage-to-two-lift-irrigation-schemes-cost-put-at-rs-700-crore)

### Stage IV — Udandapur Pump House
- **Location**: Near Udandapur village, Jadcherla mandal, Mahbubnagar district (draws from Kurumurthyraya/Karvena reservoir).
- **Number of pumping units**: **5 pumps** ("4+1") per Telangana Today; one aggregated/AI-summarized source describes "5 pumps of 145 MW" lifting to 122 m with 375 cumecs total discharge — broadly consistent.
- **Unit capacity**: 145 MW each.
- **Total installed capacity**: ~725 MW.
- **Lift height**: ~98 m by elevation difference (531→629 m); separately cited as **122 m** in one source.
- **Discharge capacity**: ~375 cumecs (≈13,240 cusecs) total cited in one source; individual pump figure of ~2,650 cusecs also reported for "Vattem and Uddandapur" jointly (source ambiguity — see conflicts).
- **Commissioned / status**: Under construction; land acquisition and R&R for Udandapur reservoir documented by Mahabubnagar district administration (Vallur & Udandapur villages affected). Not confirmed operational as of latest sources.
- **Sources**: [Telangana Today – mammoth project](https://telanganatoday.com/palamuru-rangareddy-lift-irrigation-scheme-mammoth-project-in-making); [Mahabubnagar district gov — Udandapur R&R](https://mahabubnagar.telangana.gov.in/construction-of-udandapur-reservoir-under-prlis/)

### Stage V — K.P. Lakshmidevipally Pump House
- **Location**: Near Shadnagar, Rangareddy district; final stage delivering to K.P. Lakshmidevipally reservoir (FRL 670 m), described as the terminal/highest-elevation reservoir in the scheme.
- **Number of pumping units**: Not clearly documented in available sources; total scheme figure of "31 pumps of 145 MW + 3 pumps of 75 MW" implies the smaller 75 MW units may be assigned here, but no source explicitly confirms this. **Not found in available sources.**
- **Unit capacity**: Not confirmed — possibly 75 MW units given the much smaller ~41 m lift (629→670 m) versus other stages, but this is inference, not sourced fact.
- **Total installed capacity**: Not found in available sources.
- **Lift height**: ~41 m by elevation difference (629→670 m). Not independently corroborated by a stage-specific news figure.
- **Discharge capacity**: Not found in available sources.
- **Commissioned / status**: Least-documented stage; appears to be the least advanced in construction reporting. Not found in available sources as operational.
- **Sources**: [Telangana Today – mammoth project](https://telanganatoday.com/palamuru-rangareddy-lift-irrigation-scheme-mammoth-project-in-making) (names reservoir and general project structure only).

## Data conflicts / uncertainty
1. **Source elevation**: Existing `chains.json` uses 270 m; one aggregated web-search snippet stated "+240 m" for the Srisailam foreshore. The 270 m figure is corroborated because 670−270=400 m matches the independently-sourced total lift figure and the project's own "400 m total lift" framing — treat 240 m as likely an error or a reference to a different level (e.g., minimum drawdown level, not FRL).
2. **Pump counts per stage vary by source**: Stage I cited as both 8 and 9 pumps; Stage III (Vattem) cited as 10 planned but only 4–5 installed as of a 2020 flood report. No single authoritative current tally was found reconciling all 5 stages.
3. **Total installed capacity discrepancy**: One figure (from an EIA/pre-feasibility-era search snippet) states total proposed capacity of **2,944 MW** with annual power consumption of 4,366 million units. This does not reconcile with the more recent, widely-reported unit count of "31 pumps × 145 MW + 3 pumps × 75 MW" = **4,720 MW**. This is consistent with the user's expectation of a cost/capacity escalation over time — the 2,944 MW figure likely reflects an earlier (pre-2017/2018) design stage before BHEL's 145 MW motors (bigger than Kaleshwaram's 139 MW units) were finalized for the later, larger design.
4. **Lift height per stage**: Elevation-difference-derived lift heights (this report's calculations from reservoir FRLs) do not match several directly-quoted "lift height" figures in news reports (124 m for Yedula, 121 m for Vattem, 122 m for Udandapur) — the news figures may include operating head/friction losses or use different reference levels (sump vs FRL) than the FRL-to-FRL differences calculated here. Neither set could be fully cross-verified against a primary DPR document (the environmentclearance.nic.in PDF could not be parsed as text by available tools).
5. **Discharge-per-pump figures** (2,570 / 2,650 / 3,000 cusecs) vary across articles for what should be the same 145 MW pump design — likely reflects rounding, or distinguishes rated vs. peak discharge, but sources do not clarify.
6. **Cost escalation timeline**: Original administrative approval ₹35,200 crore; ₹32,305 crore reportedly spent as of the switch of government (~2023–24); revised total estimate ₹65,506 crore as of the December-2027-deadline report. Intermediate milestones (e.g., a 2015 original estimate, or 2018 revisions) were not found in the sources retrieved.
7. **Command area breakdown**: District-wise acreage breakdown is inconsistently reported; one source gives Mahabubnagar 4,13,167 acres, Vikarabad 3,22,375 acres, Nagarkurnool 1,00,558 acres (these three alone sum to ~8.36 lakh, well short of 12.3 lakh total), with Rangareddy and Nalgonda not quantified — figures likely incomplete or drawn from an older draft EIA.
